What We Do

We are a collaborative community of experts from universities, research laboratories, and industry, united by a shared mission to create a safer and smarter digital world. By combining cutting-edge academic research with practical industry insights, we drive innovation in AI-powered digital solutions. Together, we bridge the gap between discovery and application, transforming ideas into impactful technologies that strengthen trust and resilience in the digital age.

Who We Are

We are a multidisciplinary alliance of researchers, ISP, research enters, and engineering companies dedicated to advancing innovation in the digital landscape. By uniting academic expertise with industry experience, we tackle real-world challenges through cutting-edge research and technology development. Our mission is to drive progress in areas such as cybersecurity, AI innovation, and digital infrastructure—building a connected, secure, and intelligent future together.

Our Work

Our work focuses on advancing research and innovation in cybersecurity and digital resilience. We actively contribute to European research initiatives such as the SAPPAN and PRECINCT projects, collaborating with international partners to strengthen the security and reliability of digital infrastructures. Through our studies, prototypes, and real-world applications, we explore new approaches to threat detection, incident response, and AI-driven security solutions. We regularly publish our findings to share knowledge, inspire collaboration, and drive progress across the research and industrial communities.

Our Team

Dr. Desmond Chambers

Dr. Desmond Chambers is a Lecturer at the School of Computer Science, University of Galway. His research spans cybersecurity and data analytics, with work on DDoS mitigation, IoT/network traffic management, and applications of computing in healthcare. He has published on unsupervised/self-supervised approaches for ISP-scale DDoS defence and earlier work in medical informatics and wireless sensing.
Key topics (for tags) AI/ML · Network Security · DDoS Mitigation · IoT · Traffic Analytics · Medical Informatics

Dr. Enda Barrett

Dr. Enda Barrett is an assistant Professor (Lecturer Above the Bar), School of Computer Science — University of Galway research.universityofgalway.ie He works at the intersection of machine learning, cloud and distributed computing, and cybersecurity. He has over 50 peer-reviewed publications, holds multiple patents, and leads analytics-driven research in AI and cyber defence. His earlier industrial career included enterprise-level data integration, acoustic event classification, and ontology design, and since 2015 he has focused on academic research and teaching.
Key Topics: Machine Learning · Cloud/Distributed Computing · Cybersecurity · Computer Vision · Data Analytics

Dr. Ili Ko

A researcher specializing in machine learning for network and cybersecurity applications. She has worked across academia and industry, contributing to EU Horizon 2020 projects SAPPAN and PRECINCT, where she developed host profiling and data mining frameworks to strengthen critical infrastructure resilience. Her PhD research at NUI Galway focused on ML-based DDoS mitigation and network anomaly detection. She also worked in industry as a data scientist, developing AI-driven analytics and quality assurance systems at EdgeTier. Ili’s work bridges research and practice to advance secure, data-driven systems.
Key Topics: Machine Learning · Network Security · Anomaly Detection · DDoS Mitigation · AI in Cybersecurity · Data Analytics · LLMs & NLP

Lightnet

An Irish internet service provider founded in 2006 and based in Loughrea, County Galway. The company delivers high-speed broadband solutions—including fibre-to-the-home (FTTH), VDSL, and fixed wireless—to both residential and business customers, with a strong focus on rural and semi-rural regions such as Galway, Clare, and Tipperary. Known for its reliable service and excellent local customer support, Lightnet has built a reputation for bridging the digital divide in underserved communities by offering dependable connectivity and VoIP services. The company continues to expand its network coverage across western Ireland, maintaining one of the highest customer satisfaction ratings among regional ISPs.
